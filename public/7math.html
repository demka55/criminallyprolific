Ten weeks of lessons, worked examples, and a tutor bot that makes up unlimited practice problems and walks you through the ones you miss. Covers ILM Units 2, 4, and 6.

How to use this page

One week at a time. Read the lesson, open the worked examples and try each one before looking at the steps, then work with the tutor bot until you get about ten right. That's a week.

The bot is a tutor, not an answer key. Type an answer and it tells you if you're right. Miss it and you get a nudge first, then a bigger hint, then the full solution — in that order, so you get a chance to catch your own mistake.

Your progress saves automatically in this browser, so you can close the page and come back. All ten weeks are unlocked from day one — skip around if you want to review.

Answers can be typed as decimals or fractions. 0.75, 3/4, and .75 all count as the same answer. Dollar signs and percent signs are ignored, so $12.50 and 12.5 both work.

Printing: use your browser's print command and you'll get the open week's lesson and worked examples as a clean worksheet, with the bot and navigation stripped out.
